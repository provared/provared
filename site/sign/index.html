Signing has moved

Permissions are now signed at sign.prova.red.

Open https://sign.prova.red to sign a permission or a cancellation with a passkey. This page no longer offers signing.

Why it moved. A browser lets a passkey sign only for the website name it was made for and the addresses under it. A passkey made for prova.red could also be used by any other page under prova.red. Such a page could present a record's fingerprint as an ordinary sign-in question, and have the passkey sign a record the person never saw. Nothing else is served at sign.prova.red, so a passkey made there can be used by no other page.

If you came here with a link that holds a permission (an address ending in #permission= and a long text), open the same link with https://prova.red/sign/ replaced by https://sign.prova.red/. The text after # stays in your browser and is never sent to any server.

A passkey you made here before still signs as it did, and records it signed still check. For new permissions, make a passkey at sign.prova.red, and give its thumbprint to whoever checks your records.

Questions and faults are welcome by email to hello@prova.red or as issues on GitHub.
