The demonstration

Provared, draft version 0. Evidence, not a verdict.

An office agent works under a permission that sets limits and conditions. It goes outside the permission four times. The record shows exactly where.

Everything here is invented: the supplier, the orders and the amounts. Nothing is bought, sent or deleted, and no money moves. It all runs on this computer.

  1. Give the permission

    This is the Slip: what you allow the agent to do.

    The first time, your device asks twice: once to make a passkey for this demonstration, and once to sign. If this device cannot use a passkey here, you can still check the ready-made sample record.

  2. Let the agent work

    The agent takes seven steps. It writes a Stub (a signed receipt) for each action, and the supplier countersigns its orders. For one order the agent stops and asks you first. At the end the book is sealed and time-stamped. The time-stamp service here is made up and runs on this computer.
